Why these two, and no others
Authoring is a laptop activity. Research, the note editor, the queue and the promo kit are desktop only, and a curator who opens them on a phone gets an honest message rather than a squeezed version of a professional tool.
Hosting is different. During a session the curator is watching the film like everyone else, with it on the television and this in her hand. She is an audience member with extra controls.
So the controls sit at the bottom, in reach of a thumb, and the next note sits above the chat where she can see it coming without hunting.
Same language, smaller scale. Nothing here is a new pattern.
