Projector

Pop-Up Video for the films you love, in front of the audience that already follows you.

What it is

You pick a film and set a showtime. Your audience presses play on their own copy, on whatever service they already have, and your notes arrive on their phone in time with the film, next to a chat with everyone else watching.

We never touch the film, so there are no rights to clear. Every film ever made is available to you on the first night.

How a night runs
  1. Doors, half an hour early. The room talks. Your own clips play while people wait, and your other dates take bookings.
  2. Showtime. A countdown, then everyone presses play together.
  3. The film. Your notes take the screen, then drop into the chat for anyone who was looking at their television. At the intermission you can come on camera.
  4. Five minutes after. You answer the questions people left when they booked, live. Then a banner takes the room wherever you are going next.
What you get
Seventy percent of the door

A seat is a dollar. A following of a hundred thousand converting at one and a half percent is fifteen hundred people, so a little over a thousand dollars for an evening you were going to spend talking about a film anyway.

Your conversion numbers

Every channel you post to gets its own link, so you learn which one fills a room. Your platforms will not tell you that.

Your back catalogue, working twice

The essay you already made plays to people who turned up on purpose, in the platform's own player, so the view counts where you count views.

An audience you keep

Part of the room comes from us rather than from you. At the end you take them with you.

A roster, later

Curators who join bring audiences who can see what everyone else is programming. That is worth nothing on your first night and it is not part of this offer. What being early buys is position.

A room

You are a programmer without a theatre. This is one.

The founding offer

The first five curators keep everything for three screenings. After that you keep seventy percent, which is also the standard rate.

We ask for three dated screenings, five if you are willing. You also get founding credit, introductions to streaming editorial that we broker, and your conversion data from the first night.

What we need from you is a film, a date, and one evening.
