Projector wireframes
v3 · Sep 2026
A curator programmes a night and hosts it. The audience watches the film on their own television, on a service they already pay for, and holds the app on their phone, where the curator's notes arrive in time with the film alongside everyone else in the room. The product never touches the film. It runs on a shared start time.
Page one · Audience
Thirteen phone screens at 390 by 844, in the order a night happens: finding it, the doors, the leader, the room in its three arrangements, lights up, and what is left afterwards.
Page two · Curator
Six desk screens at 1440 by 900 and two phone screens. Prep, the queue, the note editor, the dry run, on air, the promo kit, and her standing page.
The palette
Plum, crimson, gold and ivory on every screen. Teal and emerald as accents, never fills. Screen IDs match the product spec. Every name, film and number on the boards is invented sample data.
