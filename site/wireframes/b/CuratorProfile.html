The profile
The Taste Passport’s mirror. The passport is what you have sat through; this is what she has programmed.
It is what a link in her bio should open. A session page is right for one screening and wrong for a bio, because it dies the morning after. This survives between screenings and books the next one.
The back catalogue is the credential. A programmer with thirty-eight screenings behind her does not have to argue that she can fill a room.
The links are hers to choose and they are the same items she can fire into the lobby before a film. Unfurling fails often, so a preview that does not come back renders as a plain link rather than a broken card.
