# Projector

A curator-led film companion. A curator picks a film, sets a showtime, and hosts
a screening. The audience watches on their own television, on whatever service
they already pay for, and the curator's notes arrive on a phone in time with the
film. The product never touches the film.

|---|---|
| `wireframes/` | The artboard sources and `_kit.txt`, the only place a design value lives |

Read `CLAUDE.md` before changing anything. It carries what belongs in this
repository, and the conventions that keep the wireframes and the documents from
drifting away from their sources.
